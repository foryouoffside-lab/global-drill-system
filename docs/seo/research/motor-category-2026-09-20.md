# Motor drills category hub — native SEO/AEO/GEO research

**Research date:** 2026-09-20  
**Pages:** `/drills/motor` and the six localized category hubs  
**Collection:** 9 motor drills: hand-eye coordination, movement speed, keyboard testing, and precision control

## Scope and guardrails

- This is a collection page for browser-based mouse precision, aim practice, CPS/click cadence, keyboard speed, and hand-eye coordination drills.
- The page should target category intent and route users into the individual localized drill pages. It must not compete with one drill’s dedicated query by duplicating that drill’s full metadata.
- Keyword choices use native local phrasing from live SERP pages. They are not word-for-word translations from English.
- No monthly search volume, competition score, or #1 guarantee is asserted. Bing/GSC volume requires authenticated market data; Google Trends links are seeds only.
- No IndexNow, Bing submission, or pre-deployment indexing push was run.

## 14-step category SERP audit record

1. **SERP capture:** Native category and aim/precision tools are recorded above.
2. **Intent:** Collection/tool discovery intent, with sub-intents for aim practice, CPS, mouse precision, and keyboard speed.
3. **Page quality:** The category page must expose the drill list, domain grouping, and browser-first CTA immediately.
4. **Localization:** Each locale uses its own category vocabulary; no English copy is machine-translated into metadata.
5. **Title/H1/meta:** Primary precision/aim terms lead each title; the H1 and description use the same local intent without stuffing every drill keyword.
6. **Authority:** No backlink, domain-rating, or “easy competition” claim is made.
7. **Feature gap:** SkillDrills can differentiate with 9 linked interactive drills, localized labels, saved local progress, category grouping, and transparent browser-methodology copy.
8. **PAA/questions:** The existing localized FAQ blocks cover what motor drills measure, CPS, path tracing, Fitts’s Law, keyboard dexterity, browser accuracy, and training routines.
9. **Autocomplete/long tail:** Retain local modifiers around precision, aim practice, CPS, cursor control, hand-eye coordination, and keyboard speed.
10. **Feature matrix:** Match tool discovery pages with a visible collection and add scientific explanations plus direct links to each drill.
11. **Difficulty:** Category intent is broader and more competitive than a single drill; win on localized utility and clear drill segmentation, not unsupported volume claims.
12. **Volume cross-check:** Trends seeds are recorded; Bing exact-match and GSC queries remain a post-deployment validation step.
13. **Localized clusters:** The table above is the implementation source of truth for the seven hubs.
14. **Ranking probability:** Defer numeric probability until local top-10, backlinks, Bing, GSC, and trend evidence are jointly available.
