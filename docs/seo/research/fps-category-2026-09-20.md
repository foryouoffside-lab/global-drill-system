## Scope and guardrails

- This is a collection page for browser-based FPS aim training, not a replacement for any single drill page.
- Keyword choices use native local phrasing found in live regional SERP pages; they are not word-for-word translations from English.
- No monthly volume, competition score, probability, or #1 ranking claim is made. Bing Webmaster and GSC demand figures require authenticated market data.
- Google Trends URLs are regional validation seeds, not claimed trend scores because dynamic chart values are not exposed in this workspace.
- No IndexNow, Bing submission, or pre-deployment indexing push was run.

## 14-step category SERP audit record

1. **SERP capture:** Native aim-trainer and FPS practice results are recorded above.
2. **Intent:** Collection/tool discovery with sub-intents for flicks, tracking, recoil, reaction, and game-specific warm-ups.
3. **Page quality:** The hub should expose the interactive drill collection and browser-first CTA immediately.
4. **Localization:** Each locale uses its own aim-training vocabulary; no English page copy is directly translated into metadata.
5. **Title/H1/meta:** Primary local aim-training terms lead each title and H1 without stuffing every drill term.
6. **Authority:** No backlink, domain-rating, or “easy competition” claim is made.
7. **Feature gap:** SkillDrills differentiates through 15 linked drills, game-relevant mechanics, localized labels, and transparent methodology.
8. **PAA/questions:** Existing localized FAQ blocks cover transfer to tactical shooters, flick/tracking/switching, sensitivity, wrist-versus-arm aim, browser responsiveness, crosshair placement, and practice time.
9. **Autocomplete/long tail:** Retain local modifiers for game names, flicks, tracking, recoil, reaction, sensitivity, and crosshair placement.
10. **Feature matrix:** Match interactive tool intent with a visible collection, drill segmentation, and direct localized links.
11. **Difficulty:** Broad FPS aim intent is competitive; compete with native utility and clear skill segmentation, not unsupported volume claims.
12. **Volume cross-check:** Trends seeds are recorded; Bing exact-match and GSC query validation remain deployment-time work.
13. **Localized clusters:** The table above is the implementation source of truth for the seven hubs.
14. **Ranking probability:** Defer numeric probability until local top-10, backlinks, Bing, GSC, and trend evidence are jointly available.
