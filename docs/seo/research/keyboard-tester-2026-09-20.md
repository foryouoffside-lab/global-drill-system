### Audit decisions applied to all six pages

1. Candidate queries were checked with native `gl`/`hl` market pairs; the localized evidence is recorded above.
2. Intent is interactive hardware diagnosis, not a generic keyboard article.
3. Competitor UX is immediate browser testing; the existing interactive tool remains the primary above-the-fold utility.
4. Native vocabulary is preserved, including established technical loanwords such as `ghosting`, `rollover`, `NKRO`, and `chattering` where local SERPs use them.
5. Titles front-load the local test intent and stay short; descriptions state the immediate diagnostic value.
6. No backlink/domain-authority dataset was available, so no unsupported competition or ranking probability score is claimed.
7. Repeated competitor gaps are explicit explanation of event limits, layout differences, rollover interpretation, privacy, and what a browser test cannot diagnose electrically.
8. PAA data was not exposed by the connector; FAQ questions follow repeated result intent: dead keys, stuck keys, chattering, ghosting, rollover, layouts, privacy, and laptop/mobile limitations.
9. Native long-tail modifiers are used as semantic coverage, not as invented volume metrics.
10. Feature comparison: competitors provide visual key logging; SkillDrills adds localized diagnostic guidance, benchmarks/technical tiers, HowTo steps, and a full FAQ layer.
11. SERP difficulty is left unscored without authority data; the implementation targets tool intent plus specific failure-mode queries.
12. Volume cross-check is pending GSC/Bing credentials; Trends URLs are supplied for manual regional comparison.
13. Each locale receives its own primary and secondary cluster, including ABNT2 for Brazil and JIS/AZERTY/QWERTZ context where relevant.
14. A #1 probability cannot be calculated honestly from the available data. The implementation maximizes relevance, native UX, technical eligibility, and original diagnostic utility without promising a ranking position.
