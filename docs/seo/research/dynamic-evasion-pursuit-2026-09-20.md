## Drill intent

The drill follows a target moving in straight segments before it makes sudden evasive direction changes. The user must detect the visual break, make a corrective gaze shift, and resume smooth pursuit. The page serves native intent around dynamic vision, moving-target tracking, visual reaction, saccadic correction, foveal refixation, and sport/e-sport visual training. It is a browser training tool, not a clinical diagnosis or treatment.

Each locale is authored in its local search language. No English page was translated into these variants.
