# Peripheral Threat Sweeper localized SEO/AEO/GEO research — 2026-09-20

## Scope

This is an individual research pass for `/drills/physical/reflex-training/peripheral-threat-sweeper` across English, Japanese, Korean, German, Portuguese, Spanish, and French. The drill asks the user to keep a central fixation while detecting and intercepting targets arriving from the edge of the display.

No English page was translated into another locale. Existing localized guides, FAQs, scientific explanations, benchmarks, and training protocols remain intact; only the search-facing language, short drill labels, localized schema names, and freshness metadata were aligned to native market intent.

- Portuguese UFOV intent: [U-FOV on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=pt&id=app.ufov) uses native `atenção periférica`, `atenção dividida`, target detection, speed, and precision terminology.
- Spanish native intent: [Schulte Grid Trainer ES](https://schultegrid.app/es) uses `visión periférica`, stable central gaze, browser practice, reaction rhythm, and attention language.
- French native intent: [Climodo peripheral vision test](https://www.climodo.com/fr/peripheral-vision-test) uses `test de vision périphérique`, central fixation, edge stimuli, and browser training language; [SAAQ's UFOV document](https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/blob/saaq/documents/publications/espace-recherche/impact-deficits-vision-attention-performance.pdf) defines `champ visuel utile (UFOV)` around extracting information without moving the eyes or head.
- Cross-market measurement context: [UFOV Assessment](https://ufov.brainhq.com/) separates processing speed, divided attention, and selective attention, while noting that setup and display conditions affect online visual testing.
