# Mouse Tracing — native SEO/AEO/GEO research

**Research date:** 2026-09-20  
**Drill:** `/drills/motor/precision-control/tracing`  
**Markets:** Korea (`ko-KR`), Japan (`ja-JP`), Germany (`de-DE`), Brazil (`pt-BR`), Spain (`es-ES`), France (`fr-FR`)

## Scope and guardrails

- This drill measures continuous cursor/path tracking, smooth hand movement, visual-motor coordination, and staying inside a moving wave corridor. It is a skills drill, not a medical or ophthalmic diagnosis.
- Every keyword cluster below was selected from native-language search phrasing and native SERP pages. The localized pages are not English text translated word-for-word.
- No monthly search volume or guaranteed ranking position is claimed. Search results and Google Trends seeds are demand signals; volume requires authenticated Bing/GSC/Keyword Planner data.
- No IndexNow, Bing submission, or other indexing push was run.

## 14-step SERP audit record

1. **SERP capture:** Native tool/test pages and their intent language are recorded above.
2. **Intent:** Interactive tracking/precision tool first; practice and FPS mouse-control training second.
3. **Page quality:** Competitors place the tracking interaction and explanation together; SkillDrills keeps the live drill above a full guide.
4. **Localization:** Each market uses local words for tracking, tracing, following a line, cursor, and precision; no English page text was translated into the keyword list.
5. **Title/H1/meta:** Titles front-load each market’s primary cluster and keep the visible subtitle short.
6. **Authority:** No unsupported domain-rating or backlink claim is made from the live SERP sample.
7. **Feature gap:** Competitors commonly draw a path or report pointer tracking. SkillDrills adds a moving wave, continuous flow score, path-integrity telemetry, methodology, and training protocols.
8. **PAA/questions:** Existing localized FAQ blocks answer what tracking measures, how to improve smoothness, why the wave changes, and how browser input affects results; refine with post-launch PAA/GSC data.
9. **Autocomplete/long tail:** Native modifiers cover `seguir linha`, `軌跡 なぞり`, `Pfad verfolgen`, `suivre une ligne`, `선 따라가기`, and continuous tracking.
10. **Feature matrix:** SkillDrills matches browser-tool intent and provides a richer AEO answer layer plus a repeatable practice loop.
11. **Difficulty:** Long-tail tracing and cursor-control terms are plausible targets, but no “easy #1” or low-competition guarantee is made.
12. **Volume cross-check:** Trends seeds are recorded; Bing exact-match and GSC figures remain a deployment-review task.
13. **Localized clusters:** The table above is the implementation source of truth for six independent locale pages.
14. **Ranking probability:** Defer a numeric probability until country top-10 SERP, backlink, Bing, GSC, and trend evidence are available together.
