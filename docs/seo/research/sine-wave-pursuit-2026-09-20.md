# Sine Wave Pursuit — native SEO/AEO/GEO research (2026-09-20)

## Scope and intent

This drill is a browser practice page for following a target that moves in a repeating sinusoidal path. The user-facing intent is smooth visual pursuit, horizontal/vertical target following, and observing phase lag and velocity matching. It is not a clinical eye examination, diagnosis, or promise of improved vision, reflexes, sports results, or game rank.

Each locale was researched independently. Copy is authored around native search language; it is not an English page translated into the target language.

## Live research evidence

- Japan: J-STAGE research explicitly evaluates smooth-pursuit gain and phase using sinusoidal stimuli and separates smooth pursuit from saccades. Sources: [J-STAGE quantitative pursuit assessment](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jibiinkoka1947/98/4/98_4_681/_article/-char/ja), [J-STAGE predictive control with sinusoidal stimuli](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jibiinkoka1947/100/12/100_12_1450/_article/-char/ja), [J-STAGE frequency analysis](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jser1971/56/1/56_1_34/_article/-char/ja).
- Korea: the live Korean result set used `사인파`, `정현파`, and `원활추종`; Bing related suggestions also surfaced the English technical terms `sinusoidal` and `sine wave`, which were not used as the page’s primary Korean copy.
- Germany: live results support `Sinuswelle`, `Augentraining`, and Blickfolge language, while broader medical queries can drift toward eye-clinic intent. The page therefore targets practice wording and avoids medical claims. Sources: [German Augentraining guidance](https://www.audibkk-gesundheit.de/01-2018/gesund-vital/mit-augentraining-besser-sehen/), [German Augentraining and evidence](https://blog.augentraining.online/augentraining-wissenschaft/).
- Brazil/Portuguese: Brazilian sources use `perseguição suave`, `rastreamento ocular`, and target-following language; a 2026 USP dissertation also measures smooth-pursuit movement and pursuit delay. Sources: [USP gaze-estimation research](https://teses.usp.br/teses/disponiveis/45/45134/tde-14072026-142219/pt-br.html), [Portuguese visual-following terminology](https://www.institutoneurovisao.com/neuroblog/2011532_habilidade-visual-de-seguimentos).
- Spain: Spanish optometry material describes choosing sinusoidal or linear target movement for visual-following practice, supporting `movimiento senoidal` and seguimiento wording. Source: [Optonet eye-movement exercise](https://optonet.es/docs/movimientos-oculares/).
- France: French clinical research uses `poursuite oculaire lente sinusoïdale`, measures pursuit gain, and discusses horizontal and vertical sinusoidal targets. Sources: [Université de Lorraine sinusoidal pursuit study](https://docnum.univ-lorraine.fr/public/SCD_T_2008_0065_HERPIN.pdf), [Académie de Médecine pursuit definition](https://www.academie-medecine.fr/le-dictionnaire/index.php?q=mouvement+de+poursuite.), [French ophthalmology reference](https://www.em-consulte.com/em/SFO/2013/html/file_100037.html).
- Scientific framing: sinusoidal target motion is used to study smooth-pursuit gain, phase, retinal error, and predictive eye movement; results should be treated as measurement signals rather than guaranteed training outcomes. Sources: [PubMed sinusoidal pursuit measurement](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3969268/), [Journal of Neurophysiology predictive pursuit](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/10.1152/jn.2000.84.5.2340), [PubMed anticipatory eye movements](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10579198/).

## Limitations and guardrails

- Bing counts do not establish Google demand, competition, or ranking probability. Google Trends, Search Console, and Keyword Planner were not available, so no numeric high-demand/low-competition claim is made.
- The German related results were not adopted because they showed medical/local-clinic intent rather than this drill’s practice intent.
- No IndexNow or Bing submission was made. `npm run build` was not run because this project’s postbuild can submit URLs.
- SEO, AEO, and GEO improvements cannot guarantee a number-one position.
