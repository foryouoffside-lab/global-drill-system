# Predictive Pursuit — native SEO/AEO/GEO research (2026-09-20)

## Scope and intent

This page targets people looking for a browser drill that follows a moving target and estimates where it will reappear after a short visual occlusion. The page is practice and measurement content, not a clinical eye examination, diagnosis, or guarantee of sports or game performance.

Research was performed independently for each supported market. The page copy is authored in the target language around native search wording; it is not an English page translated into another language.

These clusters combine the locally used task language with the drill's actual interaction: follow, predict, and recover a target after a brief hidden interval. They are demand hypotheses, not fabricated monthly-volume numbers.

## Live checks

The local web search found relevant native terminology and supporting research:

- Japan: J-STAGE describes 滑動性追従眼球運動 and predictive control in eye movement; Japanese search results also use 動体視力 and 偏差撃ち for practical training contexts. Sources: [J-STAGE pursuit assessment](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jibiinkoka1947/98/4/98_4_681/_article/-char/ja), [J-STAGE predictive control](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/sobim/41/4/41_165/_article/-char/ja), [J-STAGE occluded-position prediction study](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/vision/21/1/21_33/_pdf/-char/ja).
- Korea: the exact Korean commercial query space was sparse in the live index, so the page uses natural Korean task wording (`가림 구간`, `궤적 예측`, `리드샷`, `동체시력`) instead of inventing volume.
- Germany: German sources use `Antizipation`, `Okklusion`, and Blickverhalten for sports-vision research, while also warning that evidence for generalized sports-vision gains is mixed. Sources: [OCL visuomotor-training review](https://www.ocl-online.de/de/des-visuomotorischen-trainings-fuer-sportler), [German Sport University gaze research](https://fis.dshs-koeln.de/de/projects/blickverhalten-in-den-sportspielen/), [occlusion research project](https://fis.dshs-koeln.de/de/projects/blickverhalten-und-antizipation-von-tr%C3%A4gheitsver%C3%A4nderten-abschlus/).
- Brazil/Portuguese: Brazilian and Portuguese sports-vision material consistently uses `antecipação`, `busca visual`, and `trajetória`; these terms were preferred over literal English phrasing. Sources: [CEV anticipation and visual-search study](https://www.cev.org.br/biblioteca/comparacao-da-antecipacao-e-do-comportamento-de-busca-visual-de-goleiros-experts-e-novatos-em-situacoes-de-penalti-com-e-sem-informacao-previa-do-batedor/), [Universidade de Lisboa sports-vision research](https://repositorio.ulisboa.pt/bitstream/10400.5/28244/1/_2023_MTAR_Bernardo%20Carapau.pdf).
- Spain: Spanish sports-vision sources use `anticipación visual`, `búsqueda visual`, and `seguimiento`; the page follows that wording. Sources: [Cuadernos de Psicología del Deporte review](https://revistas.um.es/cpd/article/view/112261), [Spanish optometry conference abstract](https://www.optomcongreso.com/abstract2024/pdf/Orales20252.pdf), [IMO sports-vision guide](https://www.imo.es/guias-y-consejos/guias-de-prevencion/vision-de-juego-guia-practica-de-salud-ocular-y-rendimiento-deportivo/).
- France: French sports-vision sources use `poursuite`, `anticipation`, `masquage`, and `suivi d une cible`; those native terms informed the page. Sources: [CNRS visual-anticipation study](https://images.cnrs.fr/photo/20220105_0006), [EVOS visual training](https://www.evos-orthoptie.com/entrainement/), [EyeMotion sports pursuit](https://eye-motion.fr/fr/sport/).
- Scientific framing: predictive pursuit includes anticipation during target occlusion, but task performance remains context-dependent. Sources: [Annual Review / PubMed predictive smooth pursuit](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31283450/), [PubMed predictive and reactive interception](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41892665/), [temporal-occlusion meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11467115/).

## Limitations and guardrails

- Bing's endpoint does not provide Google monthly volume, competition, or ranking forecasts. Google Trends, Search Console, and Keyword Planner were not available in this workspace, so no numeric “high demand / low competition” claim is asserted.
- Search engines may interpret new or niche phrases differently by market. Performance must be validated after deployment with impressions, queries, CTR, and country/language data in Search Console.
- No IndexNow or Bing submission was made. `npm run build` was intentionally not run because this project’s postbuild can submit URLs.
- SEO, AEO, and GEO improvements increase relevance and answerability; they cannot guarantee a number-one position.
