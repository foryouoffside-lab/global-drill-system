## Drill intent

The drill displays a moving target with trailing visual artifacts and asks the user to keep fixation on the target core instead of following the trail. The page therefore targets native intent around monitor ghosting, motion trails, motion clarity, visual fixation stability, moving-target tracking, and display-response checks. It is a browser training and observation tool, not a medical diagnosis of vision or a definitive panel-response measurement.

Each locale is authored around native market terminology. No English page was translated into these variants.

## Implementation decisions

- Give every locale an independent title, description, keyword set, Open Graph copy, schema naming, subtitle, guide heading, FAQ set, and related labels.
- Preserve full guide depth: scientific introduction, benchmark table, four techniques, calibration steps, cited sources, and ten distinct FAQs.
- Separate monitor artefacts from biological visual persistence: the page explains that a browser observation cannot replace a calibrated panel-response test.
- Remove visible English drill-name parentheticals and English platform labels from localized copy.
- Update localized drill names and sitemap freshness for this drill only.
- No Bing URL submission or IndexNow request was made.
