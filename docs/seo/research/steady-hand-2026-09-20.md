# Steady Hand — native SEO/AEO/GEO research

**Research date:** 2026-09-20  
**Drill:** `/drills/motor/precision-control/steady-hand`  
**Markets:** Korea (`ko-KR`), Japan (`ja-JP`), Germany (`de-DE`), Brazil (`pt-BR`), Spain (`es-ES`), France (`fr-FR`)

## Scope and guardrails

- This is a browser drill for cursor precision, narrow-path tracing, hand steadiness, and fine motor control. It is not a medical tremor diagnosis.
- Keywords below were selected from native-language pages, local SERP phrasing, and regional Google Trends seed URLs. They are not translations of the English page.
- No search-volume number is asserted. Google Trends is a relative-interest signal; autocomplete and visible SERP language are demand evidence, not monthly volume. Bing/GSC volume must be added only when authenticated exports are available.
- The current web research connector could not expose the dynamic Google Trends charts, so the six regional links are recorded as explicit follow-up seeds rather than treated as trend results. No Bing Webmaster or GSC dataset was available in this run; no volume was fabricated.
- No IndexNow, Bing submission, or other indexing push was run. The page remains an implementation candidate until deployment and Search Console validation.

## 14-step SERP audit record

1. **SERP capture:** Live native tool pages were recorded above; the research pass focused on the leading result patterns rather than inventing a volume table.
2. **Intent:** Primarily interactive tool/test intent, with a secondary training and self-benchmark intent.
3. **Page quality:** Competing pages expose the interactive test and its instructions directly; SkillDrills keeps the drill above the guide and adds a full educational layer.
4. **Localization:** Native phrasing was retained per market. No English sentence was translated into the localized keyword set.
5. **Title/H1/meta:** Primary terms are front-loaded; titles stay compact and descriptions explain the narrowing path and measurable control outcome.
6. **Authority:** No backlink or domain-rating claim is made from a visual SERP pass. This needs Ahrefs/third-party or first-party authority data before a ranking probability claim.
7. **Feature gap:** Competitor tools commonly provide a path, precision score, or steady-hand mode. SkillDrills adds progressive corridor narrowing, methodology, benchmark interpretation, protocols, and localized FAQ schema.
8. **PAA/questions:** Native question intent is represented in the existing ten localized FAQs; Search Console/PAA exports should refine them after launch.
9. **Autocomplete/long tail:** Path-following, cursor precision, steady hand, fine motor control, and mouse-maze modifiers were retained as secondary terms.
10. **Feature matrix:** SkillDrills matches the immediate browser-tool intent and adds scientific explanation, transparent limitations, and repeatable training protocols.
11. **Difficulty:** Treat as winnable long-tail intent, not guaranteed low competition. No #1 promise is made before authority and live SERP tracking are available.
12. **Volume cross-check:** Trends seeds are recorded. Bing exact-match and GSC query data are intentionally not fabricated; connect them during the deployment review.
13. **Localized clusters:** The six primary/secondary clusters in the table are the implementation source of truth.
14. **Ranking probability:** Defer a numeric probability until country SERP top-10, backlink, Bing, GSC, and local trend evidence are available together.
