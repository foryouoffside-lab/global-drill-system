# N-Back international SEO/AEO/GEO research — 2026-09-20

Scope: the existing N-Back drill only (`/drills/memory/working-memory/n-back`) and its six locale routes. No other drill was researched or generated in this run.

## Evidence and limits

- Bing Webmaster `GetKeyword` was queried with the configured project key for the exact market/language pair. Bing impressions are Bing demand, not Google volume.
- Google/Bing SERP research was used to identify native wording and current result formats. Google Trends was attempted through the public Explore endpoint for Germany, but the endpoint returned HTTP 429; no Trends score is claimed here.
- Google Search Console data was not available in the workspace, so no GSC impressions, clicks, or CTR are claimed.
- No IndexNow, Bing URL submission, sitemap submission, or other search-engine push was performed.

## SERP observations used in copy

- Japan: current Japanese app results use `Nバック課題`, `ワーキングメモリ訓練`, and `デュアルNバック`.
- Korea: current Korean app results use `듀얼 N-백`, `작업 기억력`, and brain-training/game intent.
- Germany: current German results use `N-Back Test`, `Arbeitsgedächtnis-Test`, and `Dual N-Back` for free browser tools.
- Spain: current Spanish results use `Test N-Back online`, `memoria de trabajo`, and `Dual N-Back`.
- France: current French results use `Test N-Back en ligne`, `mémoire de travail`, and `tâche N-Back`.
- Portuguese: current Portuguese results use `teste N-Back`, `memória de trabalho`, and `treino de memória de trabalho`; browser-extension and academic results show localized intent, but Bing demand is weak.
