## Scope and guardrails

This pass was completed one drill at a time. Local pages were authored around native search wording and gamer vocabulary; no English page was translated into another locale. The existing long-form guides, scientific framing, benchmark tables, calibration protocols, and bespoke FAQ sets were preserved.

The page is a moving-target aim drill whose differentiator is the second phase of the movement: flick to the target, then brake and stop cleanly. The search cluster therefore combines the broader browser aim-trainer intent with the narrower, more winnable flick-stop, mouse-precision, and overflick-correction intents.

Exact country volume, Google Trends indices, Google Search Console queries, and a defensible difficulty score were not available in this workspace. Google Autocomplete/SERP language signals are not volume measurements, and no volume or “easy #1” claim is made here. The site’s Bing credential was not usable for a country-volume export. No Bing, IndexNow, or other search-engine submission was made.

## 14-step audit record

1. Candidate queries were selected independently for each locale from native aim, reflex, stopping, and overflick wording.
2. Intent is primarily interactive tool/practice, with a secondary educational motor-control intent.
3. The page format matches the local result pattern: playable tool first, then a full explanatory guide.
4. Native SERP copy was checked for natural local terminology; no machine-translated keyword list was used.
5. Titles front-load the local primary phrase and keep the subtitle short enough to sit below the drill name without crowding it.
6. Competitor authority was not measured with a backlink provider, so no authority or competition score is fabricated.
7. The page differentiator is the visible flick-then-stop mechanic, live feedback, moving targets, and the guide’s braking protocol.
8. AEO questions are represented in localized FAQ schema and the guide’s question-shaped sections, including overflick, reaction, stopping, hardware, and scoring questions.
9. Autocomplete/SERP modifiers were used as query-shape evidence only, never as monthly volume.
10. Feature comparison: competing tools commonly expose flick/tracking/gridshot or timing; Reaction Chain adds a target-interception plus deliberate cursor-arrest loop.
11. True SERP difficulty remains unscored until country-level authority and ranking-page link data are available.
12. Bing exact-match export, GSC queries, and Trends regional indices were unavailable; this is explicitly a measurement gap.
13. Each locale has a separate primary/supporting cluster in its own script and idiom.
14. A numeric probability of reaching #1 is not claimed. The implementation targets the narrower mechanic terms because they are a more credible initial opportunity than the broad head term alone.
