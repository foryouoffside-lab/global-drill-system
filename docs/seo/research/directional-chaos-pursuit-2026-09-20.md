## Drill intent

This drill presents a free browser exercise with a target that repeatedly changes direction and velocity. The user must visually reacquire the target and make a rapid corrective gaze shift. The page therefore serves practical intent around reactive visual tracking, saccadic refixation, dynamic vision, reaction speed, and unpredictable target paths. It is a training tool, not a clinical diagnosis or treatment.

The six page variants are authored in the local search language. They are not translations of the English page.
