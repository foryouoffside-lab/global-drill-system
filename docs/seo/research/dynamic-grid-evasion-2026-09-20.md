## Search intent and page decision

Dynamic Grid Evasion is a browser interaction in which the player scans a 3×3 grid, detects a warning or danger cell, and moves the pointer into a safe cell before the hazard triggers. The native search intent is therefore a reflex/reaction test or game with peripheral and spatial awareness—not a literal translation of the English drill name.

No English page was machine-translated. Each locale's metadata, keyword cluster, and short visible subtitle was authored for its own search vocabulary while preserving the same underlying drill mechanics and guide depth.
