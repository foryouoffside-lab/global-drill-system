# Momentum Teleport Pursuit: native SEO/AEO/GEO research (2026-09-20)

## Scope and guardrails

This research supports one drill only: `/drills/visual-tracking/momentum-teleport-pursuit`. The exercise moves a visible target to a new coordinate and preserves a direction/speed cue, so its intent is rapid visual reacquisition followed by a controlled handoff back to moving-target tracking. It is a browser practice task, not a clinical saccade examination, concussion screen, vision diagnosis or promise of improved aim.

Each market was researched from native-language sources before editing. Bing keyword and related-keyword calls were read-only. They returned no exact/broad volume for the long-tail seeds below, and related results were empty or noisy. That does not establish zero demand. Google Trends and Search Console were unavailable, so demand, competition and ranking position remain hypotheses that require post-deployment validation in each local SERP.

## Live native sources

- Japan: [J-STAGE timing-response abstract](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jspehss/67/0/67_158_3/_article/-char/ja/) uses `サッケード` and a moving visual target; [J-STAGE eye movement and visual perception](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jpnjvissci/39/2/39_39.21/_html/-char/ja) describes detecting a target re-presented at a different location after a saccade.
- Korea: [Korean optometry research](https://www.dbpia.co.kr/journal/articleDetail?nodeId=NODE11527189) uses `추종 안구 운동`, `충동 안구 운동` and `양안 시기능` for eye-movement training.
- Germany: [gesund.bund.de](https://gesund.bund.de/glossar/sakkadierte-blickfolge) defines jerky `Blickfolge` and distinguishes it from smooth following; [RehaCom](https://www.rehacom.de/therapiemodule/gesichtsfeld) uses `Sakkadentraining` and compensatory visual exploration terminology.
- Brazil: [SciELO Brazil](https://www.scielo.br/j/delta/a/mSChQsvsKJkdvZPdJLnv8wh/?lang=pt) documents `sacadas`, `fixações` and `rastreamento ocular`; [Fundação H’Olhos](https://fundacaoholhos.com.br/avaliacao-do-processamento-visual/) distinguishes follow movements and saccadic movements.
- Spain: [Optonet](https://optonet.es/docs/movimientos-oculares/) separates `seguimientos` from `sacádicos` and reports response accuracy/time; [Qvision](https://www.qvision.es/blogs/patrizia-salvestrini/2014/04/06/oculomotricidad-movimientos-oculares-de-fijacion-seguimiento-y-sacadicos/) uses `fijación`, `seguimiento` and `sacádicos`.
- France: [Académie nationale de médecine](https://www.academie-medecine.fr/mouvements-oculaires-de-la-semiologie-elementaire-aux-neurosciences-cognitives/) describes saccades for rapid orientation and pursuit for maintaining a moving target; [Persée](https://www.persee.fr/doc/epc_1243-1052_2005_ant_1_1_909) describes sudden visual objects eliciting rapid saccadic orientation.

## On-page SEO/AEO/GEO decisions

- Metadata and visible copy use native market terminology for rapid target finding, fixation, saccades and the transition back to pursuit. No English page was translated into another locale.
- Answer-first content explains the drill, the difference between reacquisition and continuous following, what the metrics mean, and when to stop. JSON-LD uses localized names and current modification dates.
- Related-drill labels are localized and the sitemap receives a current override. No URL was sent to Bing or IndexNow.
