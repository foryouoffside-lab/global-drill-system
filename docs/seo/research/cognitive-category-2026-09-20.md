**Research date:** 2026-09-20  
**Pages:** `/drills/cognitive` and the six localized category hubs  
**Collection:** 8 cognitive drills covering attention, focus, processing speed, reaction time, and visual recognition

## Scope and guardrails

- This is a collection page for browser-based brain training and cognitive practice, not a medical diagnostic or treatment page.
- Keyword choices use native local phrasing from live regional SERP pages; they are not word-for-word translations from English.
- No monthly volume, competition score, ranking probability, or #1 claim is made. Bing Webmaster and GSC demand figures require authenticated market data.
- Google Trends URLs are regional validation seeds, not claimed trend scores because dynamic chart values are not exposed in this workspace.
- No IndexNow, Bing submission, or pre-deployment indexing push was run.

## 14-step category SERP audit record

1. **SERP capture:** Native brain-training, attention, memory, and reaction results are recorded above.
2. **Intent:** Collection/tool discovery with sub-intents for concentration, memory, reaction, processing speed, Stroop, and Schulte tasks.
3. **Page quality:** The hub should expose the interactive drill collection, category grouping, and browser-first CTA immediately.
4. **Localization:** Each locale uses its own brain-training vocabulary; no English copy is directly translated into metadata.
5. **Title/H1/meta:** Primary local brain-training and concentration terms lead each title and H1 without stuffing every drill term.
6. **Authority:** No backlink, domain-rating, or “easy competition” claim is made.
7. **Feature gap:** SkillDrills differentiates through 8 linked drills, measurable task scores, domain grouping, and transparent non-diagnostic methodology.
8. **PAA/questions:** Existing localized FAQ blocks cover cognitive training, processing speed, attention, Stroop inhibition, Schulte tables, transfer limits, and practice frequency.
9. **Autocomplete/long tail:** Retain local modifiers for free access, browser play, attention, memory, reaction, processing speed, and named tasks.
10. **Feature matrix:** Match tool intent with a visible collection, domain segmentation, direct localized links, and clear score methodology.
11. **Difficulty:** Broad brain-training intent is competitive and medically sensitive; compete on specific measurable tasks and cautious claims.
12. **Volume cross-check:** Trends seeds are recorded; Bing exact-match and GSC query validation remain deployment-time work.
13. **Localized clusters:** The table above is the implementation source of truth for the seven hubs.
14. **Ranking probability:** Defer numeric probability until local top-10, backlinks, Bing, GSC, and trend evidence are jointly available.
