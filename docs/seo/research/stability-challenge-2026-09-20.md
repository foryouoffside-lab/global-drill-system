## Scope and guardrails

This was handled as one drill only. Every locale keeps its own native search wording; no English page copy was translated into another language. The full interactive guide, scientific introduction, benchmark table, training protocols, and ten localized FAQ questions were retained.

The drill’s actual utility is continuous cursor/crosshair stabilization while changing force vectors push the pointer away from center. Research therefore prioritizes native terms for mouse stability, aim stability, jitter/tremor reduction, precision tests, recoil control, and browser aim training. Generic balance wording is retained only as supporting context.

No exact country search volumes, Google Trends indices, Google Search Console queries, or defensible keyword-difficulty scores were available in this workspace. Autocomplete and SERP language are demand signals, not monthly-volume measurements. No unsupported “low competition” number or #1 probability is claimed. No Bing, IndexNow, or search-engine submission was made.

## Live SERP evidence

The live results consistently frame this category as an interactive browser aim trainer or precision test. They emphasize measurable accuracy, reaction time, tracking, stability, and the use of a real mouse. This supports placing the interactive drill above a long-form AEO guide.

- Japan: [webtools-lab エイム練習](https://webtools-lab.com/tools/aim-trainer/) uses `エイム練習`, browser/no-install language, flick/tracking modes, accuracy, and average reaction time; [aimbst Japanese aim trainer](https://aimbst.com/ja/aim-trainer/) distinguishes flick, tracking, and recoil-control practice; [AimTrainer.online Japanese](https://aimtrainer.online/ja/) uses browser aim-trainer and mouse-drill wording.
- Germany: [ZeroedTools German FPS tools](https://www.dfaccount.com/de/) combines browser aim training with flick precision, target switching, and stable FPS mouse control; [Precision Aim Test DE](https://displaytestonline.com/de/mouse-test/precision-aim-test/) focuses on micro-control, click stability, and crosshair tracking.
- Brazil/Portuguese: [Sulanov Treino de Mira](https://sulanovinteractive.com/pt/tools/aim-trainer) uses `treino de mira`, mouse precision, cursor control, repeatable browser testing, and the advice to prioritize accuracy before speed; [ReflexBench PT aim trainer](https://reflexbench.com/pt/assessments/aim-trainer/) measures speed, precision, and mouse control; [Shooting Aim Trainer PT](https://shootingaimtrainer.com/pt/) uses reticle stabilization, tracking, flicking, and browser/no-download intent.
- Spain: [GameGrids Spanish Valorant trainer](https://gamegrids.io/valorant/es/training/) organizes browser practice around flick, tracking, reaction, and stable tracking; [Found Spanish aim trainer](https://found-tools.com/es/aim-trainer/) exposes static/tracking modes, average reaction time, accuracy, and live FPS; [Sulanov Spanish aim trainer](https://sulanovinteractive.com/es/tools/aim-trainer) uses mouse precision, pointer control, and steadiness before speed.
- Korea: native SERP research retained Korean FPS wording around `에임 안정성`, `에임 흔들림`, `마우스 손떨림`, `반동 제어`, and `마우스 정밀도`; noisy unrelated results were excluded rather than treated as evidence.
- France: native SERP research retained French precision/visée/stability wording and excluded unrelated vehicle or camera-stability results. The page therefore targets `visée`, `souris`, `précision`, `stabilité`, and `recul` rather than a literal translation of “balance test.”

## AEO and GEO implementation record

- Titles lead with the local stability/precision intent and keep the visible drill subtitle short.
- Descriptions answer what the tool does: keep the reticle centered while forces move it, then connect that result to jitter, recoil, and mouse precision.
- The existing ten-question FAQ schemas remain localized and question-shaped for answer engines.
- Breadcrumb, WebApplication, SoftwareApplication, VideoGame, FAQPage, and HowTo schema remain present; `inLanguage` and `dateModified` now identify each market correctly.
- Benchmarks, measurement methodology, browser/mouse limitations, and cited motor-control sources remain in the server-rendered guide.
- The sitemap contains one freshness override for the English route; localized alternates continue through the existing locale matrix.
