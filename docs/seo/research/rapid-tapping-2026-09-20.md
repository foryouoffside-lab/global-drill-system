# Rapid tapping / CPS test — native search research — 2026-09-20

## Scope and guardrails

- Drill: `rapid-tapping` / browser CPS and click-speed endurance test.
- Markets researched independently: Korea (`ko-KR`), Japan (`ja-JP`), Germany (`de-DE`), Brazil (`pt-BR`), Spain (`es-ES`), and France (`fr-FR`).
- No English-to-locale translation was used. The keyword clusters below come from native-language result pages and gamer vocabulary.
- No numeric volume claim is used here. Search-result presence and autocomplete-style phrasing show active intent, not a verified Google monthly volume. Google Trends links are regional validation paths; GSC and approved Bing Webmaster access remain the authoritative first-party checks for this domain.
- No Bing, IndexNow, or search-engine submission was performed.

## Native SERP evidence and keyword clusters

### Korea — `ko-KR`

Primary intent: `CPS 테스트`, `클릭 속도 테스트`, `초당 클릭 수`.

Supporting native intent: `마우스 연타 테스트`, `광클 테스트`, `클릭 속도 측정`, `지터 클릭`, `버터플라이 클릭`, `마인크래프트 CPS`.

The Korean results describe CPS as 초당 클릭 수 and commonly pair the generic test with 마우스 연타, 스페이스바, browser-only measurement, and gamer techniques. The page should lead with `CPS 테스트` and `클릭 속도 테스트`, while using `광클` as a native gamer modifier rather than making a medical performance claim.

Evidence: [BlockBlast Korean CPS test](https://blockblast.co/ko/click-speed-test), [ToolAct Korean click-speed test](https://toolact.com/ko/typing-speed-test), [Toolnjoy Korean CPS test](https://toolnjoy.com/click-speed).

### Japan — `ja-JP`

Primary intent: `CPSテスト`, `クリック速度 テスト`, `連打測定`.

Supporting native intent: `連打ツール`, `クリック連打`, `クリック数 測定`, `マウス 連打`, `連打力 測定`, `ジッタークリック`, `バタフライクリック`, `マイクラ CPS`.

Japanese results strongly favor 連打 and measurement language, with selectable 1–100 second windows, stability/interval analysis, mouse-versus-touch caveats, and endurance modes. The page should make the 45-second sustained target-decay mechanic explicit and use `連打測定` as a supporting phrase.

Evidence: [AnySWeb Japanese CPS test](https://anysweb.co.jp/cps-test/), [Luft Japanese rapid-fire tool](https://www.luft.co.jp/cgi/rapid-fire.php), [Attack Shark Japanese CPS tool](https://attackshark.jp/pages/mouse-click-tool).

### Germany — `de-DE`

Primary intent: `CPS-Test`, `Klickgeschwindigkeit Test`, `Klicks pro Sekunde`.

Supporting native intent: `Maus Klick Test`, `Klickgeschwindigkeit messen`, `Jitter-Klicken`, `Butterfly-Klicken`, `Minecraft CPS`, `Klick-Ausdauer trainieren`.

German results distinguish ordinary clicking from Jitter- and Butterfly-Klicken, explain best-second and consistency measurements, and warn that browser events and double-click switch chatter affect results. The page should front-load `CPS-Test` and `Klickgeschwindigkeit` without presenting online bands as population norms.

Evidence: [CPS-Test.de](https://www.cps-test.de/), [Keymap Labs German CPS test](https://keymap.io/de/cps-test/), [Tembrica German click-speed test](https://tembrica.com/de/click-speed-test/).

### Brazil — `pt-BR`

Primary intent: `teste de CPS`, `teste de velocidade de clique`, `cliques por segundo`.

Supporting native intent: `teste de clique do mouse`, `velocidade de clique`, `teste de cliques rápidos`, `jitter clicking`, `butterfly clicking`, `CPS Minecraft`, `resistência de clique`.

Brazilian Portuguese results use `cliques por segundo` as the plain-language explanation of CPS, commonly offer 1/5/10/30/60-second modes, and emphasize mouse, mobile touch, debounce, polling rate, and repetitive-strain cautions. The page should use Brazilian `clique` phrasing and keep `clicking` only for established gamer technique names.

Evidence: [Testar Periférico Brazilian CPS test](https://testarperiferico.com.br/teste-de-cps), [Super Utilitários Brazilian click-speed test](https://superutilitarios.com.br/teste-de-velocidade-de-clique/), [Adamantiun Brazilian CPS test](https://www.adamantiun.com.br/teste-cps/).

### Spain — `es-ES`

Primary intent: `test de CPS`, `test de velocidad de clics`, `clics por segundo`.

Supporting native intent: `test de clic rápido`, `velocidad de clic del ratón`, `contador de clics`, `jitter click`, `butterfly click`, `Minecraft PvP CPS`.

Spanish results separate final CPS from the best one-second burst and increasingly expose consistency, suspiciously short intervals, double-click detection, and 5/10/30-second endurance comparisons. The page should use `clics`/`clic` consistently, explain that CPS is a browser-input measure, and avoid promising game damage or an official world ranking.

Evidence: [ZeroedTools Spanish CPS test](https://www.dfaccount.com/es/cps-test), [Keymap Labs Spanish CPS test](https://keymap.io/es/cps-test/), [Pantallazo Spanish click-speed test](https://www.pantallazo.es/herramientas/click-speed-test).

### France — `fr-FR`

Primary intent: `test CPS`, `test de vitesse de clic`, `clics par seconde`.

Supporting native intent: `test de clic souris`, `vitesse de clic`, `compteur de clics`, `jitter click`, `butterfly click`, `CPS Minecraft`.

French results lead with `clics par seconde`, a large click area, personal records, multiple time windows, and a distinction between a playful benchmark and a medical or aptitude test. The page should use `test de vitesse de clic` naturally and include a clear mouse-versus-touch measurement caveat.

Evidence: [WebcamMicTest French CPS test](https://webcammictest.com/fr/mouse/cps/), [Gottrix French CPS test](https://gottrix.app/fr/test-de-cps), [MIKIRI French CPS test](https://mikiri.app/fr/cps-test).

## 14-step audit record

1. Captured localized tool SERP evidence for every market; the linked result pages are interactive CPS/click-speed tools rather than generic editorial pages.
2. Classified intent as immediate browser tool use, benchmark interpretation, and gamer technique training.
3. Compared visible UX patterns: first-click timer, selectable durations, live CPS, personal best, consistency/best-second explanations, and mobile caveats.
4. Confirmed native-language result copy for each market; retained internationally understood CPS, jitter, and butterfly terms only where native results use them.
5. Audited title/H1 patterns: primary CPS/click-speed intent belongs at the front, followed by the concrete utility.
6. Did not invent backlink or domain-authority measurements; ranking probability is treated as uncertain without GSC/Bing Webmaster/domain-link data.
7. Identified gaps to emphasize: sustained 45-second target decay, burst-versus-endurance interpretation, browser timing boundaries, and safe pacing.
8. Extracted recurring localized questions: what CPS means, what a good result means, how duration changes the result, whether mobile is comparable, and whether jitter/butterfly results are comparable to ordinary clicks.
9. Recorded native long-tail variations under each market above.
10. Feature matrix: common competitors provide short fixed timers; SkillDrills adds target decay, endurance pressure, technique context, scientific methodology, and localized guide/schema content.
11. Difficulty is assessed as mixed: established CPS tools create head terms, while the sustained-endurance and methodology angle offers a long-tail differentiation path.
12. No unverified search-volume number is used; Trends and live SERP evidence are documented, with GSC/Bing volume reserved for authenticated first-party validation.
13. Selected primary, supporting, and long-tail clusters per country above.
14. No #1 probability is promised. The implementation targets a winnable long-tail combination of native CPS intent plus sustained click endurance.
