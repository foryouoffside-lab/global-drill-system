## 3. PHASE B — LIVE RESEARCH (the part the cloud run could not do)

Rules carried from `2.md` §2 and §B3, with these specifics:

- **Sources, in this order:**
  1. Google Suggest per country and language, expanded a–z and with question words: `scripts/keywords/autocomplete.py "<seed>" <cc> <lang> --expand`.
  2. Bing volume for every candidate: `scripts/bing/bing.py keyword "<phrase>" <cc>` and `related`. A null means unknown, never zero. State the market the call resolved.
  3. Google Trends in a browser (`https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?geo=<CC>&hl=<lang>&q=<term>`), up to five terms per view, 12 months. Record rising vs falling and any launch spike, with the date.
  4. SERP capture per query in a local browser: top 10, People Also Ask, related searches, any AI answer, forum and video blocks.
  5. Local engines and communities: Naver and DC Inside (ko), Yahoo! Japan and 5channel (ja), Google.de and computerbase (de), Google.com.br (pt-BR), Google.es / .mx (es), Google.fr (fr), plus Reddit and YouTube titles.
- **Throttle:** one request every 1–2 s per host. On a captcha or rate limit, stop that host, log it, continue with another source. Never rotate IPs or evade a block.
- **Country views:** Suggest takes `gl` and `hl`, so use them. Result pages and Trends follow the laptop IP; without a VPN, label every SERP "seen from <laptop location>".
- **Labels:** every figure is `measured (source)` or `proxy (signal)`. Autocomplete is never volume. Bing volume is never Google volume. Use the wording in the Standard, Principle 3.
- **Scoring (B3):** demand, competition ease (5 = easy) and intent fit, 1–5 each. Primary keyword = best demand × ease among intent-fit phrases. Adopt 3–8 secondary phrases and the question phrases.
- **Evidence record per page:** `docs/seo/research/<locale>/<slug>.md` — primary and secondary queries, questions, intent, top-5 competitors and their gaps, PAA / AI-answer patterns, entities, trend note with date, scores, decision. Commit it with the page.
- **No evidence, no keyword.** If the tools return nothing usable for a phrase, keep the current copy and write that down.
